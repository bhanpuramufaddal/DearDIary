# Storyline arc — `director_pm_hiring`

**Window:** Sunday 2026-04-19 → Saturday 2026-05-23 (35 days). The storyline is conditional on the strategic-pivot review with David Wexler on Thursday 2026-05-28, which sits five days past window close. The hire only becomes a real loop if the platform mandate funds at that review. Inside the window, every artifact is preparatory and reversible.

## What this storyline is

If David approves the platform mandate on May 28, Aisha owes a director-level (L8) PM hire plus two senior EM hires onto the team that today is Hannah Beckwith's. The L8 PM would either be Wei Chen sliding up from L7 internally, or an external hire surfaced through the Google Workspace recruiting pod and Aisha's two ex-PM-leads at Anthropic and Figma. The Friday-morning kitchen-island writing block — 9:30 to 12:30, every Friday Aisha is home, the cleanest writing block of her week — has been quietly holding shortlist work since the second Friday in April. <!-- web-verified 2026-05-26: Anthropic product team has 30–40 PMs, led by Ami Vora; Brad Abrams (ex-Google PM) and Ryan T. are confirmed Anthropic PMs per LinkedIn -->

Inside the window, none of this is committed. The deck for May 28 is at the fourth revision. Priya has held the rehearsal slots to two. Aisha has not told her staff she is shortlisting; she has not told Wei she is considering him for the slot; she has not told the recruiting pod that the role is real. The whole storyline is an option Aisha is keeping open in the seams of a week that is structurally about something else.

## What's happened before Day 1

Aisha started the shortlist work on a Friday morning in mid-April, in a `morning_log_2026-04-10.txt` she kept on her laptop and has not opened since. The April 10 doc has the role spec in six bullets, four candidate archetypes, and a list of seven names — three internal Google L7s including Wei, two from Sasha's Anthropic network, one from Tom's Figma network, and one wildcard from Aisha's old Square cohort she has not talked to since 2018. Priya saw the file the second week of April and started building a tracker in Sheets that the two of them edit jointly. The tracker is six names long at window open; Wei is on it without his name being marked.

The pre-window context that matters: Priya is four months into the chief-of-staff role and is still establishing the rhythm of what gets surfaced upward and when. She is doing this storyline carefully because she knows it is the kind of thing where premature surfacing makes Aisha defensive. Wei has not asked Aisha about his career trajectory in eleven months and is overdue for the conversation. Sasha and Tom were both promoted past Aisha's level last year and the text thread has been nine-to-fourteen days behind since February. The Workspace recruiting pod runs hiring for four VPs and is structurally underwater on req volume.

## What could go sideways inside the window

**Maya reschedules the intake.** Recruiting-pod load is structurally heavy and Maya runs four VPs. The intake originally booked for Wednesday May 13 9 a.m. moves to Thursday May 14 9 a.m. on May 11 with one calendar update — the kind of low-friction reschedule that nonetheless eats Aisha's pre-intake prep window. Priya absorbs it. This is the within-storyline noise.

**The Sasha-floated Anthropic candidate signals he's not actually movable.** After the May 20 exploratory call, the candidate texts Sasha on May 21 that he is two weeks from a competing offer he is going to take, and Sasha texts Aisha by 7 p.m. Friday May 22 letting her know. The Friday-morning hiring-review block on the 22nd writes the slate without him in it. The candidate does not formally withdraw inside the window; the signal lands in the Sasha SMS thread and Aisha treats it as informational.

**David never gets the level signoff in.** Brendan Walsh holds the signoff request open through the week of May 18. It does not land before window close. Aisha decides on the Friday May 22 writing block to put the slate on hold until after the 28th. This does not feel like a setback; it feels like the storyline doing what the storyline was always going to do.

**The exec review goes the wrong way on May 28.** This is the silent dependency the whole storyline is hanging on. Inside the window, nothing forces it to surface. The shortlist work either becomes a real loop on May 29 or it becomes a tracker Aisha closes and does not reopen until the next planning cycle.
