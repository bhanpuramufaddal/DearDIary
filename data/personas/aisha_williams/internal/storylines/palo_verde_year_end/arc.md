Palo Verde Elementary, 3450 Louis Road, Palo Alto, K–5, PAUSD, enrollment around 334 <!-- web-verified 2026-05-26: California Department of Education school directory confirms 3450 Louis Rd address and ~334 enrollment for Palo Verde Elementary -->. Aisha and Marcus have two kids enrolled: Naima in Ms. Halverson's 5th-grade section, finishing the school and promoting to JLS in September, and Idris in Mrs. Reyes's 3rd-grade section, with two more years of Palo Verde ahead of him. ## What's happened before Day 1

Mrs. Reyes assigned play roles in the first week of April. Idris read Mrs. Reyes the Wings of Fire passage about Tsunami at the SeaWing royal hatchery during recess to get the part; this is family lore now. The PIE spring fundraiser auction night was Saturday April 5 at the Garden Court Hotel downtown; Aisha and Marcus went, Aisha wrote a $7,500 check at the silent-auction table, Karen Mendez ran the room. The 5th-grade promotion-committee planning started in March; Aisha is not on it. Ms. Halverson has been sending the Friday class note all year, and the volume is starting to tilt toward end-of-year content. Coach Petersen sent the spring-season-schedule confirmation on March 15. AYSO games have been on Saturdays at either the Greer Park fields (two blocks from the house) or Mayfield Soccer Complex; Marcus has taken all of them.

## What's expected across the window

The cadence in the warm-up days (Sun Apr 19 – Thu Apr 23) is light: a midweek ParentSquare class note from Ms. Halverson, a save-the-date for the play from Mrs. Reyes, a standing-cadence yearbook deadline reminder. On Friday April 24 Karen Mendez sends the PIE spring-fundraiser thank-you with the year's numbers, which lands in Aisha's personal Gmail and gets archived after one read.

Through the rest of April and into the first half of May the load is steady: Mrs. Reyes opens the play volunteer sign-up (Mon Apr 27), sends costume specifications (Mon May 4), sends the play call sheet and dress-rehearsal schedule (Mon May 11), and sends the Field Day volunteer ask (Tue May 12). Ms. Halverson's Friday notes carry incrementally more end-of-year logistics — the picnic, the promotion certificate, the yearbook page sign-off. Coach Petersen sends the end-of-spring-season pizza-party save-the-date Mon May 4 and the formal RSVP-and-payment email Mon May 11, with the party on Saturday May 16 at Mayfield. Karen Mendez sends the PIE board input invite for May 13 — Aisha declines politely from her phone — and a personal next-year ask on Wed May 13.

The final week is the resolution. Mon May 18 brings Principal Alvarez's school-wide end-of-year letter and Mrs. Reyes's play-Thursday-at-6 final reminder. Tue May 19 brings the time-shift update — the play moves to 6:30 because the MUR is double-booked with a PIE board meeting that runs late. Wed May 20 brings a Mrs. Reyes costume-emergency email — Idris's cape velcro tore at dress rehearsal — which Aisha handles at the kitchen island that evening with a needle and thread. Thu May 21 evening is the play; Aisha goes, Idris is great, Marcus is on a Stripe page in the back row checking his phone every ten minutes. Fri May 22 morning is Field Day; Marcus takes it. Sat May 23 morning closes the storyline with Ms. Halverson's final Friday note landing late and a quiet kitchen-island note from Aisha about what to write the Palo Verde PIE check at for next year.

## What could go sideways

The play start time slipping from 6:00 to 6:30 is the most legible disruption and the one that actually happens, triggered by the PIE board meeting overrunning in the MUR on Wed May 20; Karen Mendez is the proximate cause, Mrs. Reyes is the messenger, Aisha gets a calendar update and a clarifying email mid-afternoon Tuesday. The costume tear at dress rehearsal is the second; not catastrophic but it makes Wednesday night a kitchen-table sewing session at 9:45 PM with the cape across Aisha's lap and the laptop open beside her. The third live risk is the AYSO pizza party being pushed by a Mayfield double-booking — the soccer complex sometimes shifts club bookings without notice — which would slide the party to Sunday May 17 and put Marcus in coordination mode for a beat. Field Day weather has been holding but a wet pattern is in the forecast; the school has a rain plan but no one expects to invoke it.

Things that won't go sideways but will produce noise: yearbook order deadline spam, spirit-wear final-sale emails, AYSO fall-registration early-bird marketing, and a stray text from Coach Petersen about team-trophy logistics that Marcus handles silently.
