## In-window arc

The window is the slow drip of forms, payment reminders, and minor schedule confirmations. The shape is: a registration-confirmation cluster in the first ten days; a Camp Tawonga forms-chase that escalates twice across the window (Aisha keeps not completing the two Camper Information forms because they include a bunkmate-request item Naima has not committed to); a Mendocino balance-due reminder mid-window that becomes the largest single transactional item Aisha clears in this storyline; an AYSO summer-skills schedule change in week three that affects the drop-off window; a Galileo major-selection deadline Idris has to clear with his parents; and Renée Mensah's packing-list thread that builds toward the May 18 draft. Marcus does most of the actual replies. Aisha's contribution is approving the balance payment, signing the Tawonga forms, and writing back to Renée. There is also a steady tail of marketing-driven decoy email — iD Tech upsell emails about adding a third week of academies, Galileo "Add a week!" pitches, Tawonga merchandise emails — that Aisha glances at and archives.

## What could go sideways inside the window

The Tawonga Camper Information forms are the most likely thing to slip. Aisha has not done them because Naima has not picked a bunkmate request and Aisha does not want to write "no preference" without asking Naima. The conversation has not happened. The registrar's chase emails land politely at first and become firmer in the second pass. The Mendocino balance-due reminder is a $4,400 payment that could be missed if Marcus and Aisha both assume the other is handling it; they have done this on a smaller payment before. The AYSO mid-window schedule change moves the summer-skills drop-off from 9 a.m. to 8:30 a.m., which conflicts with Aisha's Monday staff during the first week of AYSO. Marcus will handle it but the calendar tile needs to move.
