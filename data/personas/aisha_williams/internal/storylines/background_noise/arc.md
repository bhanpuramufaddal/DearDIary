# background_noise — Aisha's storyline-agnostic mailbox floor

This storyline is the holder for everything that lands in Aisha's inbox, calendar, or phone but does not belong to a specific work or family arc. It runs the full 35-day window (2026-04-19 → 2026-05-23) as a low-frequency, low-signal floor against which the other storylines become visible. Its contents include: weekly newsletter form-emails Aisha subscribes to but rarely opens (Stratechery on Monday, Lenny's Newsletter on Sunday, The Information on Tuesday, Platformer on Friday, the NYT morning briefing); monthly SaaS and household billing notifications (Apple iCloud, Google One, 1Password, AWS personal, Schwab brokerage statement, Citizens Trust mortgage statement); recruiter cold outreach for VP-of-Product roles she will not answer; conference cold pitches (SaaStr, Web Summit); vendor cold pitches (Mixpanel, Amplitude, Pendo); Google internal-system reminders (annual security training, Workday timesheet, expense report nudges, parking permit renewal, calendar tool nudges); persona-specific decoys grounded in her actual life (LinkedIn connection requests, Palo Alto Online schools-coverage digests, PAMF appointment reminders, CVS Rx refill texts, Oura weekly summaries, Bird Dog and Tamarine reservation confirmations); and the trigger artifacts for the cross-storyline noise events declared in this phase (Priya OOO Slack, Marcus on-call text, Janelle Sunday call note, JLS portal 503 alert, Brendan biweekly reschedule). This storyline does not emerge or resolve — it is steady ambient noise the digest agent is expected to suppress unless the surrounding moments demand otherwise.
