## What could go sideways

Three things. First, the Bessemer term sheet is expected the morning of Friday May 29, six days after window close, and the gap between submission Friday May 22 and the partner-meeting verdict five business days later is the part of the process Avery handles worst. She has named the waiting problem to Dr. Reyes by name at the May 14 appointment and has not adjusted behavior since naming it. Second, an Anand Krishnan reschedule of a diligence call would not be unusual and would compress the prep window for either P2; he has already pushed one call once in the warm-up stretch. Third, Vas Natarajan at Accel may force the wind-down conversation earlier than the term sheet by sending a polite but-direct "next steps?" email, which Avery would then have to either answer or let sit; the draft has not been written and will not be written before the paper arrives.
