# Wren — Rockridge Little wrap & Park Day TK onboarding

## Scope and shape

This is the family-side parallel track of the window: Wren finishing her year in the older preschool room at Rockridge Little School on College Avenue <!-- web-verified 2026-05-25: Rockridge Little School is at 5951 College Avenue in Oakland and serves children ages 2 through 5 (Yelp, Berkeley Parents Network). --> while Avery and Sam onboard her into the transitional kindergarten cohort at Park Day School at 360 42nd Street in Temescal for fall 2026. <!-- web-verified 2026-05-25: Park Day School is at 360 42nd Street in Oakland and begins at Transitional Kindergarten (parkdayschool.org, Yelp). --> Wren turns five at the end of August, sits squarely in Park Day's TK age band, and starts on the school's first all-students day in late August. The storyline runs at a low amplitude across the whole 35-day window — a steady drip of paperwork, save-the-dates, registration prompts, and billing notices — with a small cluster of end-of-preschool moments in late May. Sam owns the action on most of it: drop-offs, forms, the parent-side logistics, and the actual filling-out and returning of the Park Day health-forms packet. Avery's role is to open every Rockridge Little or Park Day email inside the hour and to be on the thread when something requires a parent signature. The hour-rule pattern hardened during Wren's recurring ear infections in November and December 2025 — the stretch that ended with tubes placed at Kaiser in early January — and it has not relaxed in the five months since. **Persists past Day 35** — TK does not start until late August, and the July tuition installment, the August parent orientation, and the August first-day onboarding all sit on the other side of the window.

## State at window start (Day 1, Sunday April 19)

The Park Day TK acceptance arrived in late February. The $4,000 deposit was paid April 14 by ACH from the household's joint account. The handful of paperwork items that came with the acceptance — the contract, the indexed-tuition election, the FERPA acknowledgement — are signed and back to Park Day. Josie sent a "welcome to the TK family" note the week of April 13 to which Sam replied "Thank you, we're excited" and Avery did not reply. The substantive forms — the immunization record, the pediatrician-signed health form, the food-allergy questionnaire (Wren's mild peanut allergy is the thing the questionnaire wants documented), the emergency-contacts page — are all still to come. Wren's four-year well-child was at Kaiser in February; the pediatrician form Park Day needs is a separate office visit that has to happen in May or June.

At Rockridge Little, the spring is winding toward the school's two big rituals: the end-of-year program, which is the morning Annette has the older-room kids perform two short songs and read aloud from their year-end drawing portfolios for the parents, and the last day of school. Both fall in mid-June, after window close. The optional summer enrichment weeks run the last two weeks of June and the first week of July for families who want them; the Whitlock-Chens are not signed up. Sam has a teacher in-service day on May 26 (Day 38, outside the window) and is off for the summer June 6. The June 12–15 Sea Ranch long weekend is on the household calendar.

## Phasing across the window

The storyline opens with a Park Day welcome-and-calendar email from Josie on the Monday of Week 1 (Day 2, April 20). Through the rest of Week 1 and Week 2 the drip is light: a Rockridge spring-program save-the-date, a Park Day summer-camp prompt for Happy Heron, the first health-forms packet from Josie on Friday April 24 with a June 1 deadline that Sam glances at and files. The decoys begin landing on weekends — a cold-pitch from a TK reading-prep tutor, a forms-filing service that has somehow scraped Wren's Park Day enrollment, a Bay Area Parent magazine TK-camp guide promo, an Outschool transition program. Avery archives them without opening past the subject line. Sam does not see them.

The middle stretch (Days 10–22, April 28 through May 10) is mostly Rockridge — Annette sends a class field-trip permission slip for Tilden Steam Train on May 12, the optional summer-week registration window opens, Annette's spring "developmental snapshot" arrives on Tuesday May 5 (a one-page Annette-written narrative document Wren's room sends home once a semester). Park Day's contributions in this stretch are the TK supply list on Monday May 4 and the parent-orientation save-the-date on Wednesday May 6 from Angela Taylor for the evening of Wednesday August 26. Avery has already put the August 26 hold on her calendar by 9 a.m. that morning. Sam puts it on his by lunch.

The late-window cluster (Days 22–35, May 10 through May 23) is where the storyline gets dense. The Happy Heron Camp registration prompt and deadline ping land May 11. The end-of-year program logistics email goes out from the Rockridge office on Tuesday May 12. Annette's warm "Wren had a beautiful week" note lands Friday May 8 and the Rockridge end-of-year program details follow Wednesday May 13. The Park Day July tuition installment notice — a courtesy 45-day advance for $14,250 ACH on July 1 — hits Friday May 15. The Kaiser pediatrician appointment for the Park Day health-form signature is the one item that genuinely needs household coordination in the window. Avery books it Tuesday May 5 for Friday May 22 at 2:15 p.m.; on Monday May 18 Kaiser reschedules it to Thursday May 28 due to the pediatrician's CME conflict, which pushes the actual form-signing past window close. Sam picks the reschedule himself from the MyKaiser portal and texts Avery the new date inside an hour. The form deadline at Park Day is June 1, so the slip does not become an actual problem.

## Friction and risks

The structural friction is the gap between Avery's hour-rule on opening these emails and her near-zero contribution to the action they generate. Sam fills out the form. Avery acknowledges the email. The pattern is functional and has held since 2024, but in the late-window stretch where Avery is also waiting on the Bessemer term sheet and prepping the Greylock May 21 meeting, the volume of low-priority school email Avery is opening inside the hour competes for attention with the partner-meeting prep her actual work week is built around. The exposure is not that Wren's TK paperwork misses a deadline — Sam will not miss it — it is that Avery is reading each Park Day email and then closing it and then opening her phone again to read it a second time when the partner meeting starts late. This is the texture Dr. Reyes has not heard about and that does not yet rise to a flagged issue.

The contained operational risks are three: the Happy Heron Camp registration deadline (Sam needs a yes-or-no from Avery on whether they want Wren in the rising-TK camp week of June 23, which sits in the gap before Linda Chen flies down on July 6), the pediatrician form (the Kaiser reschedule pushes the appointment to May 28 but the Park Day deadline is June 1, so this is tight but not blown), and the teacher gift (Tessa wants Venmos by May 22, which is a Friday that already has fifteen other small things on it). None of these blows up. They produce small mornings in the digest, not large ones.

The secondary risk Avery has not named to anyone is the emotional shape of the wrap. Wren has been at Rockridge Little since September 2023, the room with Annette and Joelle since September 2024, and she is going to walk into a different building in August with different teachers and a different name on the lanyard. Avery has watched Wren miss her best friend Maya Khan when Maya was sick for three days in March and has registered, without acting on it, that the transition will be the biggest change in Wren's small life so far. The arc this window covers is the wrap, not the goodbye; the goodbye is June.

## Resolution

The storyline does not resolve inside the window. Wren is still in preschool the morning of Saturday May 23. The Park Day health form is still outstanding. The end-of-year program is three weeks out. The last day of school is more than three weeks out. The July tuition installment is forty days out. Park Day TK starts in late August. The shape inside the window is preparation and signal — a stack of small notifications Sam handles and Avery acknowledges, in the steady texture that has held since Wren was a baby and that the family treats as the unremarkable background of an unremarkable working spring.
