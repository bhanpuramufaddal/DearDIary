# Case study series & holdfast.co refresh — Maya Okafor

## Scope and deliverable

This is Holdfast's first proper customer case study series and the associated landing-page refresh on holdfast.co. Three written case studies — one with Lonestar Freight Group, a roughly 250-person mid-market freight forwarder out of Houston that closed in October 2025; one with Marlin Specialty Group, the mid-market specialty insurance brokerage that signed as a design partner in May 2025 on the $90K annual that is now up for renewal in the separate `design_partner_renewal` track; and one written as an anonymized case for the publicly traded regional bank that closed alongside Lonestar in October and is contractually restricted from being named in marketing collateral. The three studies follow a Challenge-Solution-Result frame <!-- web-verified 2026-05-25: Challenge-Solution-Result is the standard B2B SaaS case study framework (Concurate, Tatarek). --> with concrete metrics — hours-saved-per-month for Lonestar's ops team, close-cycle compression for Marlin's controller, an unnamed-customer paragraph for the bank — and ship together as a refreshed `/customers` landing page on holdfast.co alongside two new sub-pages, one per named customer, plus a refreshed `/case-studies` index. Target live is Thursday May 21 (Day 33) at 9 a.m. Pacific, which is Maya's commit to the team and to Avery.

The work is doubling as raise-supporting material. Mary at Bessemer's diligence team and Saam's associate at Greylock have both asked, on separate threads, whether Holdfast has published case studies they can quote. The answer through Q1 has been "in progress." Maya wants the answer to be "live on the site" by the time the term sheet arrives. The framing inside Holdfast is that the case studies are marketing collateral and the raise benefit is secondary; in Avery's head the order is roughly reversed but she has not said this to Maya.
