# Q2 board meeting prep — June 24

Holdfast's Q2 quarterly board meeting is on the calendar for Wednesday June 24, 2026, in person at the Cowboy Ventures office at 530 Bryant Street in South Park, San Francisco, 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM Pacific with lunch after. <!-- web-verified 2026-05-25: Cowboy Ventures' San Francisco office is at 530 Bryant Street in the South Park neighborhood (cowboy.vc/contact, Crunchbase). --> It is the second board meeting of 2026 — the first was January 27 — and the first one Avery has scheduled assuming she will walk in with a signed Series A term sheet in hand. The board itself is small: Aileen Lee as the sole independent director, Priya Shah and Avery as the founder seats, and one observer, James Cham from Bloomberg Beta, who has taken the board-observer chair since the seed in February 2024. The audience for the materials therefore is three people, two of whom Avery talks to every day.

Avery has explicitly held off starting board prep in earnest until the Bessemer term-sheet picture clarifies. That decision is two things at once. The first is operational: the financial pack and the cap-table waterfall section both change shape depending on whether the round closes at $14M-on-$60M, $12M-on-$52M, or slips into June and runs on Greylock paper. The second is psychological — Avery does not want to draft the narrative section twice and is willing to take the compressed three-week window between the term sheet (expected Friday May 29, six days after window close) and the board meeting (Wednesday June 24) rather than write a version of the deck she will throw out. Priya knows this and has not pushed back. Aileen knows this and has been holding off-pressure protocol correctly. The cost is that almost all the actual board-prep work falls in June, in the window between the term sheet and the meeting, and most of what surfaces inside the 35-day window is scaffolding.

The shape of in-window activity is therefore back-loaded and thin. Caroline's first scheduling ping lands Saturday May 16 (Day 28) — she sends a short email confirming June 24 is firm, attaches Cowboy's standard portfolio board-pack template (a 14-page Google Doc structure: cover, exec summary, financial pack, KPIs, product update, hiring, asks-of-board, appendix), and asks for the pre-read circulation date. Avery sees it Monday morning and replies briefly. The same Monday May 18, Marlene asks Avery and Priya by email what scope the financial section should take given the round is still open and whether the cap-table waterfall should run the pre-A or post-A scenario; Avery defers her with a one-line reply ("hold the cap table until I know what closes — pull KPIs through April") and Priya signs off on the scope question. Tuesday May 19, James Cham sends a short check-in confirming he can make June 24 from his NYC trip schedule and asking when the pre-read will arrive. Wednesday May 20, Aileen sends her one-line warm note saying she will help where useful and is happy to let the pre-read shape itself around the term sheet. None of this is urgent. All of it surfaces in the digest as track-and-FYI texture, not as decisions Avery owes anyone today.

The denser end of the window is Days 33–35 (May 21–23) and it is denser only by the standards of a storyline that is otherwise dormant. Thursday May 21 — the morning of the Greylock P2 — Marlene sends the first metrics pull for the board, asking for Avery's eyes on the ARR roll-forward by EOD Friday so she can keep her own books-close cadence on track. James Cham sends a 30-minute pre-board 1:1 ask the same morning, suggesting the week of June 15. Caroline forwards Cowboy's Q2 portfolio calendar so Avery can see where June 24 sits in the rotation. Friday May 22 — the data-room response deadline day — Marlene comes back with the cap-table scenario question, this time framed as "I need to start somewhere, give me the assumptions you want me to use." Avery defers again. James Cham's 1:1 invite arrives that afternoon for June 17 over Zoom. Saturday May 23 is Memorial Day weekend; Caroline sends one more logistics email (catering, Aileen's parking spot at South Park, observer dial-in) that Avery does not open until the Tuesday after.

What could go sideways is small and operational rather than season-altering. The most likely friction is Marlene needing the cap-table scenario locked before Avery has a term sheet — if the Bessemer paper slips by a week and Marlene's books-close window collides with the board pre-read deadline, the financial pack draft and the narrative draft both move into the last week before the meeting. The second possible friction is the pre-read date itself: Aileen would prefer it seven days out (June 17), James Cham seven to ten days out, and Caroline's template assumes five. Avery has not made this call yet and will not make it until she sees what closes. A third, smaller possibility is that the June 24 date itself moves — James Cham flagged his NYC trip in Tuesday's note and the scheduling has been firm since March, but a partner conflict on Aileen's side could push it into the first week of July, which Avery would accept with mild relief.
