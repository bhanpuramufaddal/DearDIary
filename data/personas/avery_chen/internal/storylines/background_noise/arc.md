# background_noise — Avery's mailbox & phone noise floor

Storyline-agnostic catch-all for everything that arrives in Avery's two inboxes (avery@holdfast.co and her personal Gmail) and on her phone that does not belong to any specific arc. This is the texture the digest agent must learn to suppress: SaaS billing receipts (Stripe, Brex, AWS, Vercel, Notion, 1Password, Drata) on monthly cadences; weekly newsletter form-emails (Stratechery on Tuesdays, Lenny's Newsletter on Sundays, Acquired and Pivot podcast drops, First Round Review digests, SaaStr blog); cold outreach that recurs in irregular bursts (recruiter pitches for VP Eng / Series-A-CEO roles, vendor cold pitches from sales-engagement and data-pipeline tools, conference pitches from SaaStr / Dreamforce, banker cold pitches from emerging-growth desks, founder-coaching cold pitches); internal-system reminders (Calendly weekly stats, Brex expense reminders, Rippling payroll alerts, Linear weekly project digests, Notion team-activity reports, iCloud storage nags); founder/CEO-adjacent decoys (Carta cap-table notifications, LinkedIn "you appeared in N searches", AngelList Republic monthly digest, Forge/EquityZen secondary marketplace digest, Glassdoor review pings, Y Combinator/Hacker News digest); and personal-life noise (Volvo service reminders, ACLU and Oakland Public Education Fund monthly recurring-donation receipts, Strava weekly summaries, Oura weekly insights, the block group-text alerts about lost pets and package thefts). Density is steady across the entire 35-day window with a slight uptick on Mondays and mid-week mornings. Persists across the entire window — this is mailbox texture, not an arc.
