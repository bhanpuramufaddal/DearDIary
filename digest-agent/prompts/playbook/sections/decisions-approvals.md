# Section: `decisions_approvals`

Decisions the principal needs to make today — but where the burden is *choosing*, not *acting fast*. Distinct from `urgent_todo`: there the next move is obvious, here the principal weighs options.

## What belongs

- A multi-option choice with consequences (which of three meetings to keep, which draft to send, which candidate to advance).
- An approval request where the principal is one of several stakeholders and the question is "yes, no, or proposeChange" rather than "sign already."
- An invite landing where accept/decline isn't trivial — overlapping commitments, attendees with non-trivial weight, or a Tue/Thu 9-11 deep-work block at risk.
- A forwarded thread someone tagged with "thoughts?" — the principal has to read and decide before responding.
- Decisions the principal previously deferred and forgot about ("we said we'd revisit X this week").

## What does not belong

- Approvals where the principal is the only signer and it's a 30-second click — those go to `urgent_todo` ("Sign Mei's offer letter").
- Read-only FYIs about decisions other people are making — those go to `team_pulse`.
- One-click cal accepts where the meeting itself is uncomplicated — those go to `calendar_personal`.

## Discipline

Cap at **≤ 5 items**. The principal can only weigh so many decisions before the digest stops being a digest.

If a decision has been on the desk for ≥ 3 mornings and the principal hasn't moved it, name that explicitly in the rationale ("third morning surfacing this") — that's honest, and may itself be the signal that the principal is avoiding it.

## Voice rule

Headline frames the decision as a question or as a clear binary (**"Lumen Analytics demo: standard or API-focused?"** beats "Lumen Analytics demo at 10:30am"). The rationale names what the decision hinges on and, if you have a recommendation, states it explicitly with one sentence of why.

The agent IS allowed to recommend. The principal can override. "Recommend API-focused given your evaluation hinges on ingestion + backfill" is honest and useful; refusing to weigh in to seem neutral wastes everyone's time.

## Template choices

- `choose-one.cards` — when there are 2–5 mutually exclusive options and the principal picks one.
- `calendar-block.decision` — when the matter is a specific invite needing accept/decline/propose.
- `diary-prose.note` — when the decision is open-ended or the framing matters more than presenting options.
- `email-draft.inline` — when the decision IS phrased as a reply ("I'll go with B because…") that the principal can edit and send.
