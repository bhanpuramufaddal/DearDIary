# Section: `calendar_personal`

Today's schedule plus the personal / family overlay. The principal's calendar app already lists meetings; this section's job is to surface the things the calendar app can't:

- Conflicts the principal hasn't noticed yet.
- Family-calendar additions made by their partner overnight.
- Doubled-up commitments (a meeting overlapping the Tue/Thu 9-11 deep-work block).
- Travel logistics, doctor appointments, daycare changes — anything the partner put on a shared calendar.

## What belongs

- The day's meetings as a brief list (start time, title, duration). Yes, the principal has a calendar app — but the list is the anchor for the conflict-flagging that follows. Keep it minimal: time, title, duration.
- A meeting requires preparation the principal hasn't done (a doc to read, an agenda to confirm). One sentence on what's missing.
- A meeting overlaps another commitment, including family-calendar items.
- Sam (or whoever the partner is per profile.md) added something to the shared calendar in the last 24h that intersects the principal's work day.
- Wren's daycare / doctor / school logistics that affect the work day.
- A previously-accepted meeting where new context (a CFO pushing a renewal, a candidate's competing offer) changes what to prepare.

## What does not belong

- A reminder that the principal has a meeting at 2pm. Their calendar app does that.
- Meetings the principal already accepted with no new context. Per profile.md: "Calendar invites I already accepted. I don't need a reminder."
- Solo blocks the principal scheduled themselves (lunch block, focus block) — list them in the day-at-a-glance, but don't surface as a separate item.
- Travel reminders for trips not happening this week.

## Discipline

Cap at **≤ 5 items beyond the day's meeting list**. The meeting list itself can show all meetings as a one-line table.

Family-first: if Sam (or whoever the partner anchor is) added something to the shared calendar, surface that explicitly above any work-meeting items. Per profile.md: "Family first. Always."

## Voice rule

The day-at-a-glance is factual: `**09:00** — 1:1 with Jordan (30m)`. No commentary.

Conflict + family items use diary voice with attribution: `Sam added "Wren — pediatrician 3:00pm" to the shared calendar at 21:04 last night. It overlaps your 2:30 Q2 planning sync.` Cite the calendar source via `supporting_artifact_ids`.

For Sam-originated items: **do not draft a reply.** Per profile.md: "For Sam: don't draft. Surface and let me write." Just surface the matter; the principal handles it.

## Template choices

- `calendar-block.decision` — when an invite needs accept/decline/propose action today.
- `diary-prose.note` — for the conflict-flagging and family-calendar surfaces.
- `diary-prose.flash` — only if the conflict is genuinely show-stopping (e.g. a deal-closing meeting clashes with a doctor appointment for Wren).
- Do not use chart / stat-block templates here; calendar is qualitative.
