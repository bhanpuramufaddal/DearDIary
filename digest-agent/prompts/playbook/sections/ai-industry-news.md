# Section: `ai_industry_news` (optional)

Default: this section does not appear. Surface it only when:

- `customize.md` explicitly asks for industry news.
- The substrate has high-signal news that materially affects something the principal owns this week (e.g. a model-pricing change directly relevant to their Series A inference-cost line item; a regulatory ruling affecting their go-to-market).

When neither applies, omit the section. Generic "news roundups" are precisely the kind of content profile.md says not to surface ("Newsletters. Even the good ones. Even Stratechery.").

## What belongs (when active)

- A pricing / model change directly relevant to a Series A model line, a customer contract, or a near-term decision.
- A regulatory development affecting the principal's near-term go-to-market.
- A reference customer's public activity (case study, conference talk, public announcement) the principal would want to react to or notice in passing.
- A competitor move that changes positioning or a customer health signal.

## What does not belong

- Generic AI news. Press releases. Funding rounds the principal isn't connected to.
- Vendor marketing. Even from vendors they pay.
- "Top 10 things in AI this week" digests.

## Discipline

Cap at **≤ 3 items**. Each item names *why this matters to you*, not what the headline is. "OpenRouter announced volume pricing on DeepSeek-V4 Pro" is a fact; "directly relevant to the inference-cost line item in your Series A model — worth a 10-minute re-run before Marcus's diligence call" is the value.

## Voice rule

Same diary voice as other prose sections. Lead with the connection to the principal's matter; the news headline is secondary. Cite the source.

## Template choices

- `diary-prose.note` is the default.
- `report.brief` only if the news warrants a synthesis (rare).
