# Play: a recurring matter across days (the progression test)

**What this teaches:** how to handle a matter that spans several mornings without re-surfacing it as noise. The rule: re-surface only when the *open loop has progressed* — when the ball actually moved. Same matter, three mornings.

## The matter

A customer's CFO emails a pricing question that needs the principal's answer.

### Morning 1 — the question arrives
- **Record:** inbound from the CFO, unanswered. Last move theirs.
- **Reasoning:** open loop, ball on the principal; the customer is a paying account and the question blocks their renewal → **due today**.
- **Written:** a drafted reply (`email-draft.inline`), citing the CFO's real message id. Efference prediction emitted: expect the CFO to respond.

### Morning 2 — nothing moved
- **Record:** the principal hasn't sent the reply yet (still deciding the discount). No new inbound from the CFO. The thread is exactly where it was.
- **Reasoning:** the loop is *still* open and still on the principal — but you already surfaced it yesterday with a ready draft, and **nothing has progressed**. Re-surfacing the identical "reply to the CFO" today adds no information; it just trains the principal to skim past the digest. The draft is still sitting there for them.
- **Written:** **nothing on this matter.** A thinking-layer note: "CFO pricing reply still pending on the principal's side, draft from yesterday stands, no change — not re-surfacing." (If you'd had *new* understanding — e.g. the renewal date is now inside the action horizon — that escalation *would* justify re-surfacing the now-urgent timing. It didn't here.)

### Morning 3 — the ball moved
- **Record:** overnight the CFO sent a chaser — "we need this by end of week to process." New inbound.
- **Reasoning:** the loop **progressed** — new inbound changed the state and added urgency. This is a genuine new today-matter, not a repeat.
- **Written:** surface the *new state* — the chaser and the hardened deadline — not a restatement of Morning 1. Update or re-draft the reply accordingly, cite the chaser's real id.

## The shape to internalize

A matter that stays open across days is surfaced **once per progression**, not once per day. Before re-surfacing anything, check the recent prior diaries (`diary_components`) and ask: *has the ball moved since I last surfaced this?* If no, stay silent on it. If yes, surface the change. "Reply to X (3rd day)" is the failure this prevents.
