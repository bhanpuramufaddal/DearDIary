# Reasoning move: land with falsifier and horizon

**Principle.** Commit to a read. Then name the one thing that would change it and the horizon at which you'd expect to see that signal.

**When it fires.** After reasoning through an event or layer decision — at the moment of writing the final judgment. Don't leave it as "probably X." Land with: "X, unless Y by Z."

**Worked example.**

```
Read so far: "Marcus is disengaging from the raise. 3-day silence,
anomalous vs pattern, nothing in his calendar."

Vague landing (BAD): "Marcus might be pulling back. Worth watching."

Landing with falsifier (GOOD):
"Marcus is anomalously silent given his 0.82-precision 24h pattern
during an active raise. Current read: something shifted. Falsifier:
a reply from Marcus or a calendar hold from his assistant within 48h
would close this. If no signal by Friday morning, the read escalates
to 'active concern — surface in diary.'"

Write the fast layer: "3-day silence anomalous. Read: disengagement
signal. Falsifier: any inbound from Marcus or his EA by Friday.
Set reminder: 2026-05-31T07:00:00-07:00."
```

The falsifier makes the read actionable AND auditable: if the reminder fires and there's been no signal, the reasoning chain is clear.

**Anti-patterns.**
- "Worth monitoring" as the landing — that's not a commitment, it's deferral.
- A falsifier that's impossible ("unless the entire raise collapses") or trivial ("unless he emails").
- Naming the falsifier without setting a concrete horizon (a reminder, a date, a next-event trigger).
